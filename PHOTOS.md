# Bluegrass Commercial Door & More — Real Photo Library

All photos were harvested from the live site **https://bluegrassdoor.com** (a single-page WordPress/Elementor site; every photo is used on or uploaded to the homepage `https://bluegrassdoor.com/`). The site's WordPress media library (`/wp-json/wp/v2/media`) was checked too, which is how `team-in-front-of-shop.jpg` and the full-size gallery originals were found. These are the only photos the company has published, and **none of them is larger than 1200px**. Do not upscale them past their native size. Use them with `object-fit: cover` and a navy overlay/gradient where needed.
